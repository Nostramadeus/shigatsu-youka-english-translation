## 3. Address matrix digest

CHANGES marked and their changed_from given. Source is notes/RELATIONS.tsv; the per-file translator slice
carries the individual rows, so this is the cross-file shape, not a replacement for them.
Two standing rules from STYLE.md apply everywhere: honorifics are PRESERVED as suffixes, and a drop or
change of suffix is an EVENT, not a stylistic choice.
