# How scary is it? Answers in 8 spoiler tiers

**Method + confidence.**
- Measured: every sound, picture, screen-shake, flash and video command in the decompiled scripts (`lns/`, plus the menu scripts in `orig/`), counted per file and matched against the read-through notes (`notes/SUMMARY.md`). File names and script commands only. No picture, sound or video file was opened or played.
- Inferred: "per hour" assumes about 30,000 Japanese characters of story per hour of reading. A "hard jump scare" means a sting or impact sound firing together with a horror picture that appears instantly (0-150 ms fade). The reading order is the read-through's file order, which may differ a little from the order the game unlocks things.
- Unsure: real loudness. Almost every sound is played at the engine's normal full setting, so how loud a sting is depends on the sound file itself, and I could not listen to it. Picture content is judged from file names such as "headless" or "flayed corpse" and the surrounding text, not from looking at the pictures.

---

## Tier 1: zero spoilers

- **Overall scariness for a jumpy person: 7 / 10.**

| Horror style | Rating (0-10) |
|---|---|
| Dread (slow unease) | 7 |
| Gore (blood, bodies, wounds) | 8 |
| Jump scares | 5 |
| Psychological (madness, not trusting what you see) | 8 |

- **Jump scares are occasional but steady.** They are not the main tool. The main tools are gore pictures and grim events.
- **The audio does the jolting. The pictures do the disturbing.** Muting or lowering the sound removes most of the jolt. It does not remove the pictures.
- **The useful setting:** the game has three volume sliders: BGM, 効果音 (ambient sound) and セリフ (literally "dialogue"). The game has no voice acting. About 95% of the sting and impact sounds play on the third slider, セリフ; its help text in the English build reads "Adjusts the volume of the sound effects." Turn that one slider down and the other two can stay normal.
- **Text speed barely matters.** Almost every scare fires when you click to the next page, after the text box has gone away. Manual clicking means you choose the moment. Auto-advance takes that choice away, so auto mode makes it worse, not better.

## Tier 2: what the game itself warns about (shown to every player before play)

The game shows nine notices on first boot and asks you to agree to all of them. These two are about content:

> **Unpleasant depictions.** "This work contains a very large number of unpleasant depictions and some dirty jokes. Please take care if you are not good with grotesque or frightening depictions, and with play by young children. If the player suffers any damage from this, the creators bear no responsibility whatsoever."

> **For the sake of the staging.** "For the sake of the staging, the game will sometimes force-quit. This is not a computer virus, malware, remote operation or anything else improper."

One more states that the work is entirely fiction. The other notices are not about content.

## Tier 3: how the scares are built (mechanics, no story content)

**The standard jump scare in this game:**
1. A page ends on a cut-off line, such as "I turned around, and—" or "From beneath it—".
2. You click. The text box disappears.
3. A sting or impact sound plays, and a new picture appears at once (no fade). Often the screen also shakes.
4. After a second or two, the text box returns.

**Other tools:**
- Screen shake: frequent, mostly during fights and outbursts. On its own it is a jolt, not a scare.
- Short "flash" pictures: a horror image shown for a moment, then gone. Sometimes with a whoosh sound.
- One strobe: pictures alternate every 0.05 seconds for about half a second, once. Photosensitive caution.
- Short noise or static video overlays, a few times.
- Timed cutscenes: a few scares play on a timer, not on your click. Clicking skips ahead.
- One optional screamer (a face and a scream) is in a **menu screen, not in the story**. After you have seen it once, the game offers a way to switch it off. That screamer's sound is on the **BGM** slider, not the セリフ slider.
- Force-quits: the only quit command in the scripts is in the menu system. It comes after the menu guide asks you to restart the game. I found no sudden "crash" used as a scare inside the story.

**Warning before a scare: usually a beat.** The page before a scare usually ends on a cliffhanger line, then the scare lands on your next click. A few have no warning: they open a scene or play inside a timed cutscene.

**Frequency per hour of reading:**
- Hard jump scares (sting plus a sudden horror picture): usually **1-3 per hour**. The densest stretches reach **about 5 per hour**. Some stretches have **0**.
- Smaller jolts (a bang or impact sound, often with screen shake, but no horror picture): **about 4-6 more per hour**.

**Are the early hours lighter?** Same.

## Tier 5: the scariest moments as types (no names, no places, no position)

1. A menu screen, outside the story, that turns into a classic internet screamer if you stay on it.
2. A night of ghost stories. It includes a figure in a dark garden, something at a window, and a whisper saying someone is right behind you.
3. A nightmare in which a badly injured stranger you are trying to help suddenly grabs you. Later, a very short strobe of a face.
4. A character you are following is killed suddenly, with no build-up, and the game shows it as a picture.
5. A character looks in a mirror and finds her own face changed. A dark figure with eyes keeps flashing into otherwise ordinary scenes.

## Tier 6: where it ramps (reading order split into thirds by amount of text)

| Third | Hard jump scares per hour | Horror pictures | Main flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | about 2.7 (highest) | most | ghosts, masked attackers, murders, dreams |
| Middle | about 1.6 | many | violence, madness, the most disturbing psychological content |
| Last | about 1.4 | fewest | body horror, large-scale destruction, fights (the most screen shaking) |

- The opening hours are not gentle. Graphic pictures and murder already appear in them, at about the average jump-scare rate (about 2 per hour).
- The worst stretch runs from the end of the first third into the start of the middle third. There the jump-scare rate reaches about 4-5 per hour, about twice the opening. The gore is more explicit, and the psychological content is at its heaviest.
- After that, jump scares thin out. The horror moves to large-scale body horror and fights. It gets louder (more shaking and impact sounds) but has fewer sudden-picture scares.

## Tier 7: the five scariest moments, concrete but nameless

1. **The menu screamer.** Two entries in the in-game encyclopedia are "corrupted": a violent killing and a disappearance. Open one and the panel turns red, a beep tone plays and the text becomes garbage characters. If you stay on it, after a short wait (a few seconds; I cannot time it exactly), a "scary face" image jumps onto the screen with a scream, at a random size and position. It keeps coming. The sound is on the BGM slider. After you have seen it, the menu guide offers in chat to lock it away.
2. **The ghost-story night.** A figure in a dark garden appears with an attack sting, a screen shake and screamed text, three times in a row. Then an upside-down hannya mask appears at a toilet window, with a whoosh, a shake and a written scream. Then a recorded whisper says "right behind you", and a phone photo shows an extra face. A last story ends on a breath sound and a ghost picture. Some of these are revealed a few pages later as staged by other characters.
3. **The nightmare grab, and the strobe.** In a dream, a skinless, all-red person lies moaning under a blossoming tree, and blood falls like petals. As the narrator steps back, a hand grabbing her ankle appears at once, with a heavy boom. A bone-crack sound and a screen shake follow. Later, in a different scene, the same red figure stands under a red-lit tree. A moan plays, and the screen strobes between an eye and a red face every 0.05 seconds.
4. **The instant beheading.** A scene opens with no text. A picture of a friend with blood flying shows first. Then the viewpoint character appears headless from the side. Then an impact sound and a full-screen still of the headless body. Only then does text say "It happened in an instant." It runs on a timer, so there is no click to brace on. The same sequence is reused once later.
5. **The mirror and the shadow.** During ordinary conversations, a black shadow figure with eyes flashes in with a whoosh, three times in one sequence. When the narrator asks "who are you?", a shock sting plays and the eyes zoom in. She checks a mirror, the horror sting plays, and one of her eyes has turned green. The same stretch has a stabbing seen from inside the killer's head (with a red filter and impact sounds) and a woman with no eyes who looks exactly like the narrator (the woman is text only).

Close runners-up: a heart pulled out of a character's chest from behind (picture plus a dull thud); a masked figure suddenly behind the narrator while she types on her phone; a masked body hanging from a ceiling; close-up pictures of a flayed body at an identification (heavily set up, so not a jump scare, but among the most graphic pictures).

## Tier 8: full detail, with file ids and character names

1. **Encyclopedia screamer.** Script `00001878` (the encyclopedia screen). It triggers on entries whose 特殊1 field is ビープ音. In `tsv-en/事典出来事.tsv` those are "Brutal Killing of 2 High-School Girls" (unlocked by the 'Infection' route) and the entry on Erina Goto's disappearance. Mechanics (`00001878` commands 2282-2298): a red box whose on-idle handler counts up. At 300 it plays `ビープ音` and swaps in a red cover, a red title and garbled text. At 305 or more it spawns `ワード怖い顔.gal` at a random size, rotation and position, and plays `ビープ叫び.ogg` on the BGM volume. After it is seen (`ブラクラ見た = 1`), chat topic 282 with Mikka Tachiki, the menu guide ("Scary guy in encyclopedia!!!!"), lets you lock it (`ブラクラなし = 1`, set in `000018BF`).
2. **Ghost-story night.**
   - `0000095B`: Natsumi sees a figure in the Niimura garden. `dark_attack.ogg` plays with `幸太郎幽霊.gal`, then a shake and `down1.ogg` with the text 「ぎゃあああ！」. It is Kotaro Niimura hiding as a prank.
   - `000009A0`: Kana (10) hangs upside down in a hannya mask outside the toilet window (`夏菜般若逆さ.gal`, `sceneswitch2`, shake, 「きゃあああああああ！」). She drops from the ceiling void as a prank.
   - `000009B7`: Erina Goto's story. `surprising_shock1.ogg` and `すぐ後ろだよ.ogg` ("right behind you") play with `五島携帯霊2.gal`, a photo with a second face. She admits she made the photo in advance.
   - `000009E6`: Mifuyu's station story ends on `吐息.ogg` and `女子高生幽霊.gal`.
3. **The red figure.**
   - `000001E1`: Natsumi's dream of the flayed red person under the park's sacred cherry. `つかもう.gal` and `つかむ.gal` appear instantly with `ドーン.ogg`, then `bakibaki.ogg` and a shake.
   - `0000020D`: Erina Goto on her way home sees the red person under the tree (`赤い人立ち.gal`). Then `うめき声.ogg` plays and a strobe runs: `夢の目` / `赤顔` / `桜道夜赤` at 50 ms each. The figure is Natsumi's father, Ryoji Kogori, the flayed victim. At the end of that file Erina is struck down from behind.
4. **Instant beheading of Natsumi.** `00000488` opens on a timed cutscene: `五島血が飛ぶ.gal`, then `なつみ首なし横.gal`, then `down1.ogg` with the full-screen `なつみ首なし.gal`, then 「ほんの一瞬の出来事だった。」. It is reused in `00000EDF` (block 208, where Natsumi's head is gone in an instant in front of Erina).
5. **Erina Goto's mirror and shadow, `00000EDF`.**
   - `五島影目.gal` flashes in with `sceneswitch2` three times during normal talk.
   - `surprising_shock1.ogg` plays with `五島影目` zooming to `五島影目拡大`.
   - The mirror check plays `怖い効果音.ogg` with `緑目.gal` (her eye has turned green).
   - She stabs her senpai and then Kana with `hit_axe1` sounds and `殺人フィルター.gal` (the killing urge). This is a nightmare she wakes from.
   - The eyeless woman who looks like Erina is text only.
   - This file has the most hard jump scares of any file (10). Neighbouring files `00000E7B` (8), `00000F24` (5) and `00000EF6` / `00000F0D` form the densest cluster.

Runners-up with ids:
- `0000156A`: Haruka pulls out Natsumi's heart (`心臓取り出す.ogg`, `なつみ心臓.gal`).
- `00000F24`: a white hannya figure appears behind Haruka while she uses her phone (`白般若シルエット.gal`, `down1.ogg`); a masked figure hangs from the ceiling (`死神首つり.lcm`); the eye-removal picture (`目玉取り出す.gal`).
- `00000538`: Natsumi identifies her father's flayed body (`皮剥ぎ死体1-3.gal`, each shown for about 2 seconds with a whoosh).
- `0000212D`: turning around to a masked 死神 (`surprising_shock1.ogg`).
