Voice verdict

  • A keeps the JP shape more often: noun-ending lines stay noun-ending, the bare もし refrain stays bare, あの人 stays "that person" (row 36: "I as good as killed that person.").
  • B adds small emphasis the JP does not have ("If only", "long and hard", "and nowhere else"), which is the ADDITION failure mode in small doses.
  • B follows the owner's comma ruling (O-30) and keeps the "used to" in rows 23/79/184; A does neither.
  • Both are equally readable; neither has a clearly unreadable line.
  • A reads more like a human translator following the doctrine; its faults are mechanical and fixable in one pass.

Ship
